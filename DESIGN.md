## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, institutional-grade commercial ecosystem linking wholesale distributors and fast-moving retail merchants across Tajikistan. It balances the robust utilitarian reliability of enterprise inventory management with the swift, tactile certainty of modern financial infrastructure.

### Design Movement: Modern Precision Commerce
The aesthetic blends **Modern FinTech Precision** with **Functional B2B Architecture**. The interface eschews decorative frivolity in favor of structural clarity: crisp hairline delimiters, purposeful micro-surfaces, compact typographic density, and unequivocal visual feedback. It conveys liquidity, stability, logistical velocity, and absolute transparency.

### Tone & Sensibility
- **Unyielding Trust & Legibility:** Financial balances, SKUs, inventory tiers, and invoice statuses must be legible under glaring sunlight in roadside retail stands or dimly lit logistics terminals.
- **Operational Cadence:** Dense, highly functional, and low-friction. Interactions trigger crisp feedback without bloated layout shifts.
- **Localized Authority:** Native accommodations for Tajik Somoni (`TJS` / `смн`), bilingual script agility (Latin/Cyrillic), and prominent physical hand-off verifications (one-time physical delivery tokens, security clearance stamps).

## Colors

The chromatic structure emphasizes institutional solvency, precision logistics, and immediate state recognition.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0F766E` - Deep Teal / Emerald):** Anchor of growth, transaction completion, and verified enterprise legitimacy. Applied to primary calls-to-action, active stage lines, positive margins, and official security validation checkpoints.
- **Secondary (`#1E293B` - Deep Slate):** Grounded structural slate. Drives table heads, structural sidebars, high-emphasis typographic anchors, and administrative boundary frames.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Ochre):** Operational alert and interim state signifier. Applied to pending clearances, orders under assembly, and financial holds.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Neutral):** Structural mid-tones, secondary metadata, borders, inactive indicators, and ledger grid lines.

### Functional Order Status Colors
- **NEW:** `#2563EB` (Cobalt) | Background: `#EFF6FF` | Border: `#BFDBFE`
- **CONFIRMED:** `#0284C7` (Sky) | Background: `#F0F9FF` | Border: `#BAE6FD`
- **ASSEMBLING:** `#D97706` (Amber) | Background: `#FFFBEB` | Border: `#FDE68A`
- **IN_TRANSIT:** `#7C3AED` (Violet) | Background: `#F5F3FF` | Border: `#DDD6FE`
- **DELIVERED:** `#059669` (Emerald) | Background: `#ECFDF5` | Border: `#A7F3D0`
- **DISPUTED:** `#DC2626` (Crimson) | Background: `#FEF2F2` | Border: `#FECACA`

### Surface Strategy
- **Base Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50) for reduced eye strain during extended inventory scans.
- **Surface Elevation 01:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with an explicit `#E2E8F0` hairline boundary.
- **Surface Contrast (Ledger / Dark HUD):** `#0F172A` (Slate-900) for night routes, courier verification screens, and financial roll-up headers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy separates structural layout communication from transactional and data-heavy ledger reading.

### Font Pairings & Roles
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Modern, sharp, and confident. Provides an authoritative fintech edge across top-level views, metrics, and supplier storefront names.
- **Body & Controls (`Inter`):** Systematic and neutral with exceptional Cyrillic and Latin script legibility. Prevents fatigue in high-volume catalog ordering and logistics tables.
- **Data, Ledgers & Codes (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to numeric quantities, SKU hashes, tabular currency values (`TJS`/`смн`), invoice references, and customer pickup security pins. Tabular alignment ensures decimal numbers align across deep vertical ledgers without shifting columns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an enterprise fluid grid that transitions dynamically between mobile field operations (courier app and shopkeeper terminal) and expansive desktop views (distributor ERP, bulk ordering dashboards).

### Grid Disciplines
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Optimized for touch-driven single-hand interactions, high visual density, bottom-docked validation actions, and sticky summaries.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Facilitates split-screen operation: order manifests on the left, live security verification and route progress on the right.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with a maximum container boundary of `1440px`, centered with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Designed for multi-column ledger tracking, complex filtering matrices, and catalog management.

### Vertical Rhythm
All heights, paddings, and margin increments adhere strictly to an **8px base grid** (subdivided into 4px micro-spacers for badges, borders, and input internals). This preserves visual order and ensures reliable vertical stacking across dynamic font scales.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a **surface-first tonal layering model** with hairline technical outlines. Heavy, ambient shadows are minimized to maintain a utilitarian, data-dense look that performs well on low-spec mobile devices.

### Depth Layers
- **Floor (Canvas):** Base background `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Tier 1 (Cards, Ledger Containers):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an explicit `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. No shadow is applied under neutral conditions; hierarchy is driven by surface contrast.
- **Tier 2 (Sticky Headers, Interactive Rows, Active Filters):** `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #CBD5E1` and micro-elevation: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Action Bars, Delivery Verification Blocks):** `#FFFFFF` with `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` and a subtle primary ring border (`1px solid rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.15)`).
- **Tier 4 (Modals, Confirmation Sheets, Security Code Inputs):** Full dismissive scrim `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` supporting a white surface elevated with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (Level 1)** geometry (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radius), creating an engineered, disciplined appearance. This low-radius aesthetic aligns with invoice lines, structured data sheets, and physical delivery waybills.

### Radius Implementations
- **Base Components (`rounded-sm` = 2px to 4px):** Checkboxes, table row selections, nested inline tags, and status dots.
- **Standard UI (`rounded` = 4px):** Standard buttons, text field inputs, product quantity steppers, and inline alerts.
- **Structural Surfaces (`rounded-md` = 6px to 8px):** Ledger cards, invoice modules, bottom sheets, and modal frames.
- **Exceptions (Full Pill):** Exclusively reserved for status chips (`rounded-full`) to immediately differentiate contextual metadata from actionable rectangular buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#0F766E` with white text, font `label-lg`, 4px radius, 40px height on desktop, 48px on mobile for finger-target compliance. Hover: `#115E59`. Active: `#134E4A`.
- **Secondary:** Surface white with `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive:** Bordered or solid `#DC2626` for handling disputes and order cancellations.
- **Courier Action (PWA Bottom Anchor):** Full-width, 52px tall, primary green with inset verification badge icon.

### Status Badges (Pill Shape)
- Composed of uppercase `label-sm` tracking, `px-2 py-0.5`, rounded-full, with an inset `6px` solid status beacon dot.
- Example: **IN_TRANSIT** utilizes background `#F5F3FF`, text `#6D28D9`, border `1px solid #DDD6FE`, dot `#7C3AED`.

### Ledger Cards & Balance Blocks
- Dual-surface design: The upper deck holds the creditor/debtor summary and balance in `Space Grotesk` (`28px`), with `смн` notation in `JetBrains Mono`.
- The lower deck holds reconciliation metrics on an alternating micro-tint surface (`#F8FAFC`), divided by a hairline border (`#E2E8F0`).

### Delivery Security Verification Block
- High-contrast visual unit for physical goods turnover between courier and retailer.
- Dark backdrop (`#0F172A`) containing a `verification-pin` field (`4-6 character numeric code` in `JetBrains Mono`, letter spacing `0.15em`, color `#34D399`).
- Features a high-contrast QR scan button and an instant "Confirm Handoff" trigger.

### Order Status Timeline Widget
- Linear, segmented pipeline indicator (`NEW` → `CONFIRMED` → `ASSEMBLING` → `IN_TRANSIT` → `DELIVERED`).
- Completed nodes show `#0F766E` with a checkmark; the current node features a pulsing ring animation (`rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.2)`); pending nodes remain `#E2E8F0` with slate numerals.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- Input frames feature an explicit `1px solid #CBD5E1` boundary, transitioning to a sharp `2px solid #0F766E` on focus.
- Wholesale order inputs pair numeric text boxes directly with physical case/box multiplier chips (`×10`, `×50`, `×100`).
